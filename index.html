FAQs

Common Questions, Straight Answers

Curious about service areas, response times, or how quotes work? Start here.

How quickly can a tech get to Thornbury for a no-heat call?

Same-day in most cases. A local tech covers Thornbury and the Grey County communities around it, the village core and rural properties toward Clarksburg and Ravenna included, and we treat a no-heat call as the priority it is.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, fully licensed and insured, with certified technicians across Thornbury and The Blue Mountains who handle gas furnaces, propane and oil setups, heat pumps, and every major brand.

My cottage runs on propane or oil. Can you service it?

Absolutely. Many properties around Thornbury heat with propane or oil, especially out toward Clarksburg, Ravenna, and the orchard lots, and our technicians service those furnaces and boilers alongside the high-efficiency gas units common in town.

Are heat pumps a good fit for Thornbury homes and cottages?

Yes. Cold-climate heat pumps handle heating and cooling in one efficient system, which suits both year-round homes and cottages around Thornbury. We size the unit to your actual load and handle the rebate paperwork.

What's your fee to come out and diagnose a problem?

Installations get a free, no-obligation quote, and repairs carry a straightforward diagnostic fee. We walk you through every cost before lifting a wrench, so the invoice holds no surprises.

Do you work on all brands of furnace and AC?

Yes. Our certified technicians service, repair, and maintain all major furnace and air conditioner brands found in Thornbury and Grey County homes, century harbour houses and new builds alike.

Do you offer financing on new equipment?

Yes, we offer financing options on installations like heat pumps, furnaces, and mini-splits. Ask us about current plans when you request your free quote.

Do you serve areas outside of Thornbury?

Yes. We serve Thornbury and the nearby Grey County and South Georgian Bay communities of Clarksburg, Craigleith, Ravenna, Meaford, and Collingwood, plus the rural and cottage properties in between.
